## What was tested

The question was whether a bank’s proposed correction follows the **original evidence available on the reporting date**. That includes preserving accurate history before the incident, extending the review after the bank’s end date, recognizing when cure becomes observable and making a supported decision about each current account field. Continued processing also has to protect the previous decision if incoming evidence is stale. ## Scope and sign-off

The study uses synthetic observations. The next original numeric month after a severe event is a reversal proxy. Servicing records establish actual reversal, causation, closure and delinquency onset. Actual bureau submission, acknowledgment, subsequent reporting and the resulting consumer file require independent evidence. The largest native run covered 25,000 accounts. Windows PowerShell 5.1 compatibility was reviewed in source. The recorded runs used PowerShell 7.7.0 on Linux. A single database snapshot cannot authenticate concurrent changes to every saved copy of a valid incident date. Compare a retained authoritative source when available. [Operational responsibilities](BUSINESS_REQUIREMENTS.md#br-13)

CDIA is the official source. The public 2020 Credit Reporting Resource Guide, printed pp. 4-10, 4-11, 4-15 and 4-16, supports the historical field examples. The current 2026 field pages require authorized access and were outside this validation. The model lacks consumer bankruptcy or receivership indicators and their associated dates. A missing required rating at 05 or 13 can leave the historical date condition unresolved. The current authorized guide and applicable source records govern an actual submission. [CDIA Metro 2 resources](https://www.cdiaonline.org/resources/furnishers-of-data-overview/metro2-information/) · [2020 field definitions](https://autodealerplus.com/dealerzone/metro2.pdf#page=64) · [Historical Field 25 hierarchy](https://autodealerplus.com/dealerzone/metro2.pdf#page=70)

**Imported-rating perimeter.** The missing-rating screen examines null and blank values where the inspected historical standard requires 17B. A nonblank `X` or `D` at status 13 can pass this scoped check. Its conditional Field 25 requirement remains unresolved. Before applying the sandbox to imported data, validate these values against the applicable 17B code set and obtain servicing evidence for unsupported ratings. The final auditor found zero such cases at historically required statuses in the reference populations. The field model does not assign an onset in their place. [Historical 17B definitions](https://autodealerplus.com/dealerzone/metro2.pdf#page=64) · [Authoritative current access](https://www.cdiaonline.org/resources/furnishers-of-data-overview/metro2-information/)
